OFFICIAL REPORT // FILE #047

THE BLACKWOOD MURDER

Initial police briefing and crime scene survey of Blackwood Manor.

CASE #047: BLACKWOOD, V. STATUS: UNSOLVED
METROPOLITAN POLICE // 4TH PRECINCT Homicide Incident Summary // Date: Oct 2, 23:55 EST
CONFIDENTIAL
VICTIM: Victor Blackwood (Age 54, Industrialist)
LOCATION: Blackwood Manor, Master Study (2nd Floor North)
ESTIMATED TOD: Approximately 11:47 PM (Corroborated by watch)
CAUSE OF DEATH: Blunt force trauma to the occipital skull
PRIMARY ANOMALY: Study door bolted from inside; north window unlatched

INCIDENT NARRATIVE

At approximately 11:52 PM, housekeeper Martha Higgins alerted police after hearing a violent thud and shattered crystal coming from Victor Blackwood's private study. The heavy solid oak door was locked with an internal heavy brass slide bolt which cannot be engaged or thrown from the outside corridor.

When officers breached the room at 11:58 PM, Victor Blackwood was discovered prone beside his mahogany desk. A vintage gold pocket watch had broken upon impact, its hands frozen at 11:47 PM.

THE INVESTIGATOR'S CRUCIAL PUZZLE:
While the study door was bolted from the inside, the north-facing casement window overlooking the rose trellis was discovered unlatched and swinging open in the gale. Fresh mud and soil traces were spotted across the window sill. The killer either entered or escaped via the garden trellis.
